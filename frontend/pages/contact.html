Got Questions?

Here are some common questions about TypeMaster.

What is TypeMaster?

TypeMaster is an online platform designed to help users improve their typing speed, accuracy and keyboard skills.

Is TypeMaster free to use?

TypeMaster provides free typing practice features so users can work on improving their typing skills.

How can I improve my typing speed?

Practice regularly, focus on accuracy, maintain proper finger placement and gradually increase your typing speed.

Can beginners use TypeMaster?

Yes. TypeMaster is designed for beginners as well as users who want to improve their existing typing skills.

How can I report a technical problem?

Use the contact form above and select Technical Support to describe the issue.
